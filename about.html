摄影师 · 夏黎YuiTari

关于

我拍摄星空、风光与夜色中的大地，也通过「星野开源」分享拍摄和后期的方法。

现为星联 CSVA 星空摄影师、夜空中国供稿人、中国国家天文供稿人、风光 pixel 供稿人；同时是 NiSi 特约地标摄影师、视觉中国签约摄影师、图虫签约供稿人，以及 Getty Images / iStock 签约供稿人。

I photograph the night sky and the landscapes beneath it, and share my capture and processing methods through the “Open Nightscape” series.

I am a CSVA astrophotographer and a contributor to Night China, Chinese National Astronomy, and Fengguang Pixel. I also work with NiSi as a featured landmark photographer and contribute to Visual China, Tuchong, and Getty Images / iStock.
