让星轨渐渐隐去

从“取最大值”出发，用一张年龄图记录星轨的时间，让堆栈更轻、更快。

Starting with a maximum stack, an age map tracks each trail’s passage through time and makes the process lighter.

夏黎YuiTari · 星野开源

阅读小红书原笔记 ↗

普通星轨堆栈通常对每个像素“取最大值”：保留所有照片中最亮的值。轨迹因此清晰，却也容易显得生硬。渐隐星轨希望保留明亮的头部，让更早的尾部逐渐变暗。

为什么重新设计堆栈

最初，我想使用 TIFF 素材制作星轨。Photoshop 会产生很大的中间文件；使用达芬奇处理大量高分辨率素材时，速度和导出稳定性也不理想。传统滑动窗口每次都回看最近的一段照片，星轨越长，重复计算越多。

两张图，逐帧迭代

新的方法只保留当前累计的星轨图和一张“年龄图”。年龄图记录每个像素的残影已持续多少帧：更亮的新星点出现时，年龄归零；没有新亮点时，年龄增加，旧轨迹便向尾部移动。

为不同年龄预先计算一张衰减表。运行时按像素查表更新亮度，不必为每个输出帧反复处理整个时间窗口。其计算量接近普通最大值堆栈的量级，内存压力也较小。

记录星点的年龄，再决定它该如何淡去。

可以调节的质感

  • 轨迹持续长度，决定尾部大约保留多少帧；
  • 渐隐曲线，决定亮度下降的速度；
  • 最终剩余亮度与轻微模糊，控制画面的收尾与柔和程度。

不同参数会形成不同的视觉语言。真实感与艺术化之间如何取舍，仍由作品的表达目的决定。

A conventional star trail stack keeps the brightest value at each pixel across the sequence. The result is clear, but the trails can feel hard. A fading trail keeps its head bright while older parts gradually recede.

Why change the process?

I wanted to work with TIFF files, but Photoshop created very large intermediate files. Resolve was also slow with many high-resolution frames and could stall during export. A sliding-window method revisits recent frames for every output image, so longer trails demand more repeated work.

Two images, updated frame by frame

The revised method keeps only the accumulated trail image and an “age map”. Each pixel’s age returns to zero when a brighter new point appears; otherwise, its age increases as the older trace moves toward the tail.

A decay lookup table is computed in advance. During processing, the age value selects the appropriate brightness without revisiting the whole window. This brings the work close to the cost of a standard maximum stack, with less memory pressure.

Track the age of the light, then decide how it fades.

Shaping the result

  • Trail duration determines how many frames the trace remains visible.
  • The decay curve controls whether the tail fades slowly or quickly.
  • Final brightness and a slight blur influence the softness of the image.

Different settings produce different aesthetics. The balance between a documentary rendering and artistic interpretation depends on the intent of the image.
